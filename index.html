Frequently asked

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time through our Jane booking page, where you can choose a service, a practitioner and a time. You can also call our front desk at 905-503-9191 and we’ll be happy to book for you.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed to book with us. For example, a referral or previous diagnosis is not required to see our chiropodist. Some insurance plans ask for a doctor’s note before they’ll reimburse certain services, so check with your plan administrator.

Do you offer direct billing?

Yes, we offer direct billing (conditions may apply). We also direct bill Blue Cross and Canada Life for military and RCMP veterans. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, debit and cash.

Is shockwave therapy covered by insurance?

Shockwave services are covered under physiotherapy, chiropractic and chiropody benefits. Treatment usually takes 3 to 4 sessions at weekly intervals, and each session lasts 5 to 10 minutes.

Does cold laser therapy cost extra?

No. Our practitioners incorporate cold laser therapy into treatments at no additional cost.

Do you treat children?

Yes! We enjoy working with people of all ages, from newborns to 90-year-olds. Chiropractic care, chiropody, massage and physiotherapy can all be tailored to children and teens.

Who performs acupuncture and dry needling?

Our Registered Acupuncturist and TCM Practitioner, Victor Cao, offers acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine. Our chiropractors and physiotherapists are also trained to perform acupuncture and/or dry needling, and Dr. Carano offers cosmetic facial acupuncture.

Clinic policies

24-hour cancellation & missed appointment policy

Your appointments are reserved especially for you. When an appointment is forgotten, cancelled or changed without enough notice, we miss the chance to fill that time and other clients miss the chance to receive care. We respectfully ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule.

Appointments missed, rescheduled or cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice are subject to a $60 cancellation fee. Appointments cancelled or missed during peak season and popular appointment times may be subject to 100% of the appointment fee. Missed appointments cannot be charged to your extended health benefits.

Late policy

Out of respect for everyone’s time, please arrive on time. If you arrive late, you’ll be treated for the remaining time available and the regular booked fee will apply. If fewer than 10 minutes remain, you won’t be treated; it will be considered a no-show and the cancellation fee will apply.

Refund policy

We do not offer refunds for any tests, products or supplements. Once a product is purchased and billed, refunds and declined billings on patients’ behalf with insurance companies are not possible. Please find out your coverage before booking your appointments.
